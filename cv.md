**Peak array index KATA from CODEWARS:** _In this kata you should simply determine, whether a given year is a leap year or not. In case you don't know the rules, here they are: Years divisible by 4 are leap years, but years divisible by 100 are not leap years, but years divisible by 400 are leap years. Tested years are in range 1600 ≤ year ≤ 4000._

```
function isLeapYear(year) {
  return year >= 1600 && year <= 4000 && (year % 400 === 0 || (year % 4 === 0 && year % 100 !== 0))
}
```
